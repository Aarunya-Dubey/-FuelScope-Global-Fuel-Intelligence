03 / About the project

Making energy data easier to understand.

FuelScope brings together global fuel-price and taxation indicators to help people explore the forces behind energy costs. The interactive dashboard is supported by three story-led views designed for different decision-making perspectives.

Inside the dataset

The project examines country-level fuel prices and related economic indicators across 2020–2026.

  • Petrol, diesel and LPG price comparisons
  • Fuel taxation and subsidy indicators
  • Country, region and income-level comparisons
  • Trends designed for policy and business analysis
